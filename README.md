Some of my recent projects:
  - [cecileane/simulation-ratevariation](https://github.com/cecileane/simulation-ratevariation): Simulations to assess the effect of substitution rate variation on hybridization summary statistics, determining most
methods tested have high type-1 error in the face of lineage-rate variation. Published in [Systematic Biology](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/syad056).
  - [laufran/f4-ratevar](https://github.com/laufran/f4-ratevar): Simulations to evaluate the accuracy of inferring complex f4-based admixture graphs, finding small cycle size negatively impacted ability to infer the true graph. Published in [Genetics](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/iyaf259).
